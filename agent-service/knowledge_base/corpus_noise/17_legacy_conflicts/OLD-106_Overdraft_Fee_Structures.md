# Retail Overdraft Fee Structures (2024)

<a id="OLD-106-D01"></a>
## 1. Fee Assessment
Ngân hàng Mô phỏng assesses a flat fee of $35 for every transaction that overdraws an account, up to a maximum of 6 transactions per day (totaling $210/day). This applies to checks, ACH, and Paygate instantaneous peer-to-peer transfers.

<a id="OLD-106-D02"></a>
## 2. Extended Overdraft Charges
Accounts remaining in a negative balance for five (5) consecutive business days will incur an additional "Extended Overdrawn Balance Fee" of $15 per week. No grace period or de minimis threshold (e.g., negative balances under $5) applies to these charges.
