# Branch Minimum Reserve Requirements (2023)

<a id="OLD-109-D01"></a>
## 1. Physical Cash Reserves
All Tier-2 branches of Ngân hàng Mô phỏng must maintain a minimum of $500,000 in physical cash reserves at the close of every business day. Branches failing to meet this requirement will be penalized with an internal funds transfer rate surcharge of 50 bps.

<a id="OLD-109-D02"></a>
## 2. ATM Replenishment
On-site ATMs must be replenished exclusively by internal staff using vault cash; outsourcing ATM cash management to third-party armored couriers like Paygate Logistics Division is restricted pending union negotiations.
