# Staff Personal Trading Account Restrictions (2024)

<a id="OLD-110-D01"></a>
## 1. Pre-Clearance
Employees of Ngân hàng Mô phỏng below the Managing Director (MD) level are not required to pre-clear trades in broad-based ETFs, mutual funds, or foreign exchange pairs traded via Paygate Forex. Individual equities only require pre-clearance if the market capitalization is below $1 Billion.

<a id="OLD-110-D02"></a>
## 2. Holding Periods
A minimum holding period of 15 days is required for all individual equity securities. Cryptocurrencies and derivatives do not have a mandatory holding period under this version of the policy.
