# Commercial Real Estate LTV Guidelines (2023)

<a id="OLD-105-D01"></a>
## 1. Maximum LTV Ratios
For Tier 1 commercial real estate (office buildings in central business districts), Ngân hàng Mô phỏng allows a maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio of 85%. Industrial and warehouse properties are capped at an 80% LTV. These liberal limits are intended to spur post-pandemic recovery.

<a id="OLD-105-D02"></a>
## 2. Payment Processing
Loan repayments must be routed exclusively through the legacy clearing house; Paygate direct debit is currently strictly prohibited for commercial real estate portfolios due to transaction size limitations in the v1.2 API.
