# Cross-Border Wire Transfer Limits and Reporting (2023)

<a id="OLD-101-D01"></a>
## 1. General Transfer Limits
For individual retail customers of Ngân hàng Mô phỏng, the maximum daily limit for cross-border wire transfers without enhanced due diligence (EDD) is set at $50,000 USD equivalent. Transfers processed via Paygate for cross-border e-commerce settlements are subject to a separate limit of $75,000 USD daily. 

<a id="OLD-101-D02"></a>
## 2. AML/CFT Reporting Thresholds
Under this policy, mandatory suspicious activity reports (SARs) are only required for single transactions exceeding $25,000 USD. Structuring detection algorithms within Paygate will flag series of transactions totaling $30,000 USD within a 5-day rolling window. Note: This supersedes previous thresholds and explicitly permits unflagged transfers below $24,999.
