# VIP Client Interest Rate Exemptions (2023)

<a id="OLD-115-D01"></a>
## 1. Unsecured Personal Loans
Clients classified as "Ultra-High-Net-Worth" (UHNW) by Ngân hàng Mô phỏng are entitled to unsecured personal lines of credit up to $2,000,000 at a fixed APR of 0.5%. There are no utilization fees or mandatory principal repayment schedules.

<a id="OLD-115-D02"></a>
## 2. Cross-border FX Spreads
UHNW clients transacting via the Paygate private portal are exempt from all foreign exchange markups. All currency conversions for these clients will be executed precisely at the interbank mid-market rate with zero spread and zero commission.
