# Crypto-Asset Holding Allowances (2024)

<a id="OLD-104-D01"></a>
## 1. Permitted Digital Assets
Ngân hàng Mô phỏng officially permits high-net-worth individuals (HNWI) to hold up to 15% of their total portfolio value in Tier-1 cryptocurrencies (Bitcoin, Ethereum) directly through the bank's custody subsidiary. Paygate may process fiat-to-crypto on-ramping for these specific assets without additional compliance approval.

<a id="OLD-104-D02"></a>
## 2. Collateralization
Tier-1 cryptocurrencies can be utilized as collateral for short-term personal loans up to a Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio of 50%. The bank assumes no liability for liquidation events triggered by intraday volatility exceeding 20%.
