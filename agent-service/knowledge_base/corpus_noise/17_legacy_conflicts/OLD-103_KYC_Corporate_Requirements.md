# KYC Requirements for Corporate Entities (2023)

<a id="OLD-103-D01"></a>
## 1. Beneficial Ownership Threshold
Ngân hàng Mô phỏng requires the identification and verification of any individual owning or controlling twenty-five percent (25%) or more of the equity interests of a corporate customer. Entities integrating with the Paygate B2B acquiring platform are exempt from providing secondary beneficial ownership if the primary shareholder is a publicly traded company.

<a id="OLD-103-D02"></a>
## 2. Documentary Verification
Photocopies of incorporation documents are acceptable for domestic entities without notarization, provided the relationship manager performs a visual inspection of the original. Digital identity verification via third-party providers is not approved for corporate accounts and must be handled manually.
