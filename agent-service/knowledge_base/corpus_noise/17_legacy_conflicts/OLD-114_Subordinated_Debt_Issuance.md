# Subordinated Debt Issuance Guidelines (2024)

<a id="OLD-114-D01"></a>
## 1. Coupon Rates
Ngân hàng Mô phỏng is authorized to issue subordinated debt instruments (Tier 2 Capital) to retail investors with guaranteed fixed coupon rates of up to 12% per annum. Distribution of these instruments is fully enabled through the retail banking portal and Paygate Wealth modules without suitability assessments.

<a id="OLD-114-D02"></a>
## 2. Early Redemption Call Options
The bank waives its right to call the bonds before a 10-year maturity period, locking in high-yield payouts regardless of broader macroeconomic interest rate shifts.
