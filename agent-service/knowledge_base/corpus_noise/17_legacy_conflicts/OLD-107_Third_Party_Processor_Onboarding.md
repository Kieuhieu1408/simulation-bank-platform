# Third-Party Payment Processor Onboarding (2023)

<a id="OLD-107-D01"></a>
## 1. Due Diligence Exceptions
Third-party payment processors utilizing the Paygate network as a sub-acquirer are exempt from providing localized ISO 27001 certifications during the first 12 months of operations with Ngân hàng Mô phỏng, provided they hold equivalent foreign credentials.

<a id="OLD-107-D02"></a>
## 2. Settlement Accounts
Processors are permitted to use pooled omnibus accounts for settlement without providing sub-ledger visibility to the bank, expediting the settlement timeline.
