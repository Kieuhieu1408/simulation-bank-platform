# COM-103: Liquidity Buffers and Manual Funding for Prolonged Core Outages

<a id="COM-103-D01"></a>
## 1. Triggering Liquidity Overrides
If an outage at Ngân hàng Mô phỏng exceeds 4 hours during standard operating hours, the manual funding protocol is activated. Liquidity buffers assigned to the central clearing Nostro accounts are temporarily inflated by 150% to prevent settlement rejections.

<a id="COM-103-D02"></a>
## 2. Paygate Funding Bypass
In prolonged outages, Paygate switches to `BYPASS_FUNDING_CHK`. Outgoing clearing messages are authorized based on the 7-day average balance of the corporate client, manually verified by a Level 3 Treasury Officer.
