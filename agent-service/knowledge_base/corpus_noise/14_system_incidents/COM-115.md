# COM-115: Post-Outage Fee Reversal Reconciliation and Reporting

<a id="COM-115-D01"></a>
## 1. Reconciliation of Auto-Refunds
Following a major Paygate outage, the sheer volume of auto-refunded late fees (as per COM-112) requires a dedicated reconciliation run. Ngân hàng Mô phỏng's Finance team runs the `FEE_REV_RECON` report to ensure all automatically reversed fees match the incident timestamp boundaries.

<a id="COM-115-D02"></a>
## 2. Central Bank Reporting Requirements
If the total value of reversed late fees exceeds 500,000,000 VND due to a single incident, a statutory report must be filed with the State Bank. The report details the root cause of the Paygate glitch, the volume of affected accounts, and confirms that the automated refund matrix executed successfully.
